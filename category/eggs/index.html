Farm fresh · Nutrient-rich · Naturally protected

Enjoy truly farm-fresh eggs, produced the way nature intended. Our pasture-raised eggs are left unwashed to preserve their natural protective layer, known as the bloom.

This natural coating seals the shell's pores, helping to:

  • Keep bacteria out
  • Reduce moisture loss
  • Keep the eggs fresh longer, naturally

Conventional store-bought eggs are washed, which strips this layer away. Ours keep their original quality, taste and shelf life.

Premium hen diet, superior eggs

Our hens are raised on open pasture and fed a carefully balanced organic, nutrient-rich diet, including:

  • Organic barley
  • Pure-Life sea protein
  • Organic vitamins and minerals
  • Essential oils
  • Probiotics
  • Carrot enzymes

The result is eggs that are:

  • Richer in flavor
  • Higher in nutrients
  • Naturally wholesome

Why choose Udder Milk eggs?

  • Pasture-raised for the best quality

  • Unwashed, so they stay fresh longer
  • Produced with care and transparency
