Pastured Chicken

PasturedHalal

Out on grass every day, eating bugs, worms and greens, with farm-grown feed on the side. No confinement barns. Hand-slaughtered and halal.

Pasture-raised chicken, hand slaughtered and halal

Our chickens are raised on fresh pasture and moved regularly onto new grass, where they forage for grasses, bugs and worms. In summer they spend up to 15 hours a day outside, which makes for healthier birds and richer flavor.

Chickens naturally need some grain and minerals, so along with pasture they get a balanced feed of farm-grown grains, field peas, sunflower meal, fish meal, oats, calcium and poultry minerals. Our farmers grow much of that feed themselves.

Why our chickens are different

  • Pasture-raised on grass every day
  • A natural diet of grasses, bugs and worms
  • Hand slaughtered
  • Halal
  • Farm-grown feed
  • No industrial confinement
  • Rich flavor and better texture
  • Raised by local family farmers

Naturally yellow fat

You may notice deep yellow skin and fat, especially in spring, summer and early fall. That's what plenty of pasture and a diet rich in grasses and plants looks like.

Fresh in summer, frozen in winter

To keep quality high all year, our chickens are usually sold fresh in the warmer months and frozen in winter.

About soup chickens

Soup chickens are former laying hens, about a year old. The meat is leaner and needs slow cooking to become tender.

  • Simmer for 12 to 18 hours
  • Use a crock pot or slow cooker
  • Excellent for rich homemade broth and soup

If you'd like tender meat that cooks in about an hour, choose a whole roasting chicken instead.
