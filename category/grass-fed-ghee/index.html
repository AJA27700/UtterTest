Ghee Residue (Morta)

Traditional

The soft, toasty, paneer-like bits that settle at the bottom of the pan when ghee is made. Rich in protein. Spread it on toast, bake with it, or use it in sweets.

When ghee is made, cream is churned into butter and the butter is melted down. The clear golden liquid is the ghee. The soft, creamy, brownish layer that settles at the bottom of the pan is the residue, called morta or mava. It's rich in protein and fat.

How to use it

  • Spread it on toast or bread
  • Knead it into bread dough, or bake it into cookies
  • Make quick sweets from it with just a few ingredients
