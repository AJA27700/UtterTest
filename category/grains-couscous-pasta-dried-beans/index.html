Egyptian Couscous

$8.00

Fine couscous. Make it savory, or sweet with butter, sugar and nuts.

Savory couscous

Ingredients: 1 cup fine couscous, ¾ cup boiling water, 1 tbsp olive oil or butter.

  • Mix the couscous with the oil or butter in a bowl.
  • Add the boiling water a little at a time (you may not need it all), and let it rest 5 minutes.
  • Fluff with a fork, salt to taste and serve.

Use chicken or vegetable broth instead of water for extra flavor. Serve with grilled chicken, beef or sautéed vegetables, or add chickpeas, herbs and lemon juice for something lighter.

Sweet Egyptian couscous

Ingredients: 1 cup fine couscous, ¾ cup hot milk (or water), 1 tbsp butter, 1 to 2 tbsp sugar or honey, raisins or nuts if you like.

  • Mix the couscous with the butter.
  • Add the hot milk a little at a time and let it rest 5 minutes.
  • Fluff with a fork, sweeten to taste and serve warm. Add cinnamon, vanilla or coconut for extra flavor.
