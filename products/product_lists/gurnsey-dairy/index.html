Raw Cultured Guernsey Butter

Price at login

RawA2/A2GuernseyCultured

Cream cultured overnight, then churned. Tangy and deeply buttery.

How we make it

  • We warm raw Guernsey heavy cream to 77°F.
  • We add MM 100, a live, active culture, and hold the cream at that temperature for 24 hours, in stainless steel only.
  • The cultured cream then sits in ice water for 12 to 24 hours, until it's cold all the way through.
  • Once it's thoroughly cold, we churn it into butter.

The culturing gives it a deeper, slightly tangy flavor, like the butter on an old farmhouse table. Enjoy it on good bread first.
