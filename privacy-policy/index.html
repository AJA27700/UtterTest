Who we are

Udder Milk Creamery Association (“Udder Milk,” “we”) is a private members' association at 2960 Nolo Rd, Penn Run, PA. You can reach us at info@uddermilk.com or 973-413-9585.

What we collect

  • When you join: your name, email address, password, phone numbers and delivery address, which we confirm on a map.
  • When you order: what you order, your delivery day and zone, your order history, and any notes you send us.
  • When you contact us: whatever you choose to include in your email, call or text.

We don't collect card numbers. Orders are paid in cash on delivery. If we refund you by Venmo or Zelle, we use only the details you give us for that refund.

How we use it

  • To run your membership and your account.
  • To prepare, deliver and confirm your orders, and to handle credits and returns.
  • To contact you about your orders and deliveries.
  • To send you promotional messages by phone, which you agree to when you sign up. Tell us any time and we'll stop.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. We share it only with the people who help us deliver your order, with the services that host our website and member system, and when the law requires it.

Cookies

The member system uses cookies to keep you logged in. This website remembers one setting in your browser, the “In stock only” shop filter, and nothing else.

Your choices

You can update your details in your account at any time. To close your account or ask us to delete your information, email info@uddermilk.com.

Children

Our membership is for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
