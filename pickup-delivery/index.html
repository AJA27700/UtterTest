Pickup & delivery

From the pasture
to your porch.

We deliver milk 12–24 hours after milking, so some deliveries arrive late in the evening. Here's how to make delivery day easy.

Your delivery day.

  1. Find your day and cut-offLog in and open the Delivery Day tab. It shows your delivery day and the date your order needs to be in.
  2. Put out a coolerWe don't give exact delivery times. If you'll be out, leave a cooler with ice by the door. An 11-quart cooler fits most orders.
  3. Leave your cash insidePayment is cash on delivery. We don't take cards or checks.
  4. Look for us between 11am and midnightIf we can't make your regular day, we'll deliver the next day and let you know by email. Want a rough window? Email us and we'll tell you.
Delivery window

11am to midnight

About 1 in 10 orders arrive after 10pm.

Why so late?

We try to deliver your milk 12–24 hours after milking. The late-night stops are the price of that freshness, and most members think it's worth it.

Where we deliver

Our routes cover the area on our delivery map. Log in to see your delivery zone. Pickup spots and times are listed in your account too.

Order status

Open, closed
or confirmed?

Open

We have your order, and you can still change it.

Closed

Your order is locked in and can't be changed. It will come on your delivery day.

Confirmed

Same as closed. You'll get a confirmation email, and it's on the truck for your day.

Changing or canceling

Cancel before you get your confirmation email. To cancel, log in, open My Cart, and delete your items one by one until the cart is empty.

If that doesn't work, email the cancellation to info@uddermilk.com with your full name, address and the name of your zone.

Missed deliveries

If we don't get a cancellation before cut-off, nobody's home and there's no cooler with payment, or the driver can't get into your building, you'll be charged 50% of the undelivered order on your next order.

Returns

  1. Email us about the itemTell us what's wrong. We want to know.
  2. Keep it coldRefrigerate it if it needs the fridge, freeze it if it needs the freezer.
  3. Hand it to your driver next weekYou'll get credit on the spot. Drivers can't give credit without the item.

Something missing, or need to swap an item? Reorder it and subtract it from your next order's total. You can also email us for a refund by Venmo or Zelle.

Picking up instead?

Pickup spots and times are listed in your account. If your pickup order is under the minimum, just add the $15 minimum order adjuster.
